# Earnings Analysis

## Overview

Turn a fresh earnings print into a sharp research readout. Focus on what was new in the quarter, what beat or missed expectations, what management said, and whether the thesis changed.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the event.
   Verify the company, quarter, release date, and whether the relevant materials are current.
2. Measure the print.
   Compare reported revenue, EPS, margins, and key operating metrics against consensus and prior guidance.
3. Explain the drivers.
   Identify which segments, geographies, products, or cost lines drove the outcome.
4. Assess guidance and tone.
   Note changes to full-year or next-quarter outlook and any management commentary that shifts conviction.
5. Translate to the stock.
   Decide whether the result changes estimates, thesis, risk-reward, or only near-term positioning.

## Minimum Deliverable

- Revenue, EPS, and key KPI beat or miss summary
- Guidance change summary
- 3 to 5 bullets on what mattered in the quarter
- Explicit statement of thesis impact: improved, unchanged, or weakened
- Next step: maintain view, update model, or revisit valuation

## Questions to Answer

- Was the quarter a beat or miss relative to what mattered most?
- Was the quality of the beat strong or driven by lower-quality items?
- Did guidance reinforce or undermine the prior setup?
- Which metric is most likely to drive the stock reaction?

## Red Flags

- Guidance unchanged despite a weak operating print
- EPS beat driven by tax, buybacks, or one-offs while core KPIs disappoint
- Segment mix deterioration hidden by consolidated growth
- Management commentary that conflicts with reported trends

## Handoff

If the quarter changes forward assumptions, run `model-update` immediately after this skill.
