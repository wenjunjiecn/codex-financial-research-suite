# Morning Note

## Overview

Draft a fast, opinionated note for the morning meeting. This product should be readable in a couple of minutes and should surface only what is actionable.

## Workflow

1. Scan overnight developments.
   Focus on earnings, guidance, M&A, management changes, regulatory news, major macro prints, and meaningful pre-market moves.
2. Pick the lead.
   Decide the one headline the audience most needs to hear first.
3. Add quick takes.
   Summarize relevant company and sector events in one or two lines each with a view, not just a recap.
4. Flag today’s catalysts.
   List earnings calls, data releases, conferences, or other time-sensitive events.
5. Close with action.
   State the positioning implication, trade idea, or "nothing material changed" if appropriate.

## Minimum Deliverable

- One lead call
- Overnight developments section
- Key events today section
- Optional trade idea section
- Tight, readable format suitable for email or chat

## Writing Standard

- Lead with judgment, not chronology
- Keep each item concise
- Make clear what matters versus what is noise

## Red Flags

- Listing headlines without saying why they matter
- Burying the lead under lesser news
- Turning a morning note into a full research report

## Handoff

If one company update deserves fuller treatment, route to `earnings-analysis` or `model-update`.
