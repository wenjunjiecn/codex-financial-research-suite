# Idea Generation

## Overview

Generate candidate ideas systematically. Treat screens as a sourcing tool, not a conclusion, and make sure every surfaced name comes with a reason it may be mispriced.

## Workflow

1. Define the hunt.
   Confirm direction, market-cap range, sector scope, geography, and style or theme.
2. Screen for candidates.
   Use valuation, growth, quality, short-risk, or special-situation logic depending on the mandate.
3. Add thematic context.
   For theme-driven work, map the value chain and separate direct beneficiaries from crowded or indirect ones.
4. Triage the list.
   Narrow to the names where valuation, narrative, and catalyst line up best.
5. Present the shortlist.
   For each name, explain why it may be mispriced, what unlocks value, and what could prove the idea wrong.

## Minimum Deliverable

- Clear screen criteria
- Shortlist of candidates
- One-line thesis for each
- Key risk for each
- Suggested next step: deep dive, model build, or pass

## Screening Lenses

- Value: cheap on earnings, cash flow, or asset backing
- Growth: durable topline with improving unit economics or margins
- Quality: returns, balance sheet, and consistency
- Short: deteriorating fundamentals with stretched valuation
- Special situation: spin, restructuring, activist, or event-driven setup

## Red Flags

- Surface-level screens with no catalyst
- Crowded themes where every obvious winner is already fully priced
- Short ideas without timing or downside catalysts

## Handoff

Use `sector-overview` for thematic context or `comps-analysis` and `dcf-model` for deeper work on the best candidates.
