# Sector Overview

## Overview

Build a sector landscape that explains market structure, key players, growth drivers, competitive dynamics, and valuation context. The point is to help the user understand where value accrues and where the best debates live.

## Workflow

1. Define scope.
   Pin down the sector, subsector, geography, and whether the request is neutral landscape work or thesis-driven.
2. Size the market.
   Summarize market size, growth, segments, and the main assumptions behind those figures.
3. Map the structure.
   Explain value chain, consolidation level, business models, and barriers to entry.
4. Profile the leaders.
   Compare the major public players on scale, growth, margins, positioning, and valuation.
5. State the debates.
   Identify the central bull and bear arguments, likely catalysts, and where the market may be too complacent or too excited.

## Minimum Deliverable

- Market structure summary
- Key secular tailwinds and headwinds
- Competitive landscape table
- Sector valuation context
- 3 to 5 investment implications or open debates

## Red Flags

- Repeating TAM claims without clarifying what is actually addressable
- Mixing incomparable business models in the same "peer" framing
- Treating the sector as static when regulation or technology is shifting the boundary

## Handoff

For name-level follow-up, move into `comps-analysis`, `dcf-model`, or `idea-generation`.
